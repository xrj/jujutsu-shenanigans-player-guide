Video research, not a patch guarantee

Use gameplay videos as dated evidence

Gameplay is often easier to understand by watching, but a video is tied to its recording date. Use it to see menu flow, movement rhythm, or a Build Mode example; do not copy a title or thumbnail as proof that a method still works today.

A usable source

These Builds keep getting Better | Jujutsu Shenanigans is a community gameplay reference used in the material ledger. Its value is visual discovery of a player workflow, not authoritative balance information.

Turn a video into safe notes

  1. Write down the upload date and the feature shown.
  2. Cross-check any named feature with a current game or update source.
  3. Summarize the player task in your own words instead of copying subtitles or descriptions.
  4. Link to the original video; do not download and rehost it without permission.

What to test after watching

Test only one observable action: can you still open the menu, find the option, or reproduce the basic workflow? If the answer is no, the video belongs in a historical reference list, not a live guide.
