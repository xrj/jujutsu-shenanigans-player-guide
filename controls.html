Use the controls as a practice checklist

  1. Test the block key without trying to learn a combo at the same time.
  2. Practice dashing and returning to guard.
  3. Only then try a character page or a community mechanic guide.

What this page does not claim

This page does not claim exact timing windows, damage values, combo guarantees, mobile layouts, or version-specific interactions. Those require current in-game testing or a clearly dated authoritative update note.
