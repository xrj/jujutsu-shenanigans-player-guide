Link verification

Find the right community link

Search results can show Discord and Trello links, but this local build does not label any link “official” unless it can be re-confirmed from a first-party game channel. That is especially important when invites expire or names are copied by unrelated communities.

Verification order

  1. Open the current Roblox game page.
  2. Check whether the social area or developer-controlled profile links to the same destination.
  3. Compare the server/board identity with a second reputable reference.
  4. Record the date you checked it; do not recycle old invitation screenshots.

Useful leads, not final proof

Discord inviteCommunity destination to verify through current Roblox-linked channels before calling it official.
Trello/Discord articleThird-party discovery lead; not enough by itself to establish ownership or permanence.

Keep player safety in the page design

Never ask players to download executables, paste scripts, share account details, or follow a link that cannot be cross-checked. A link-verification page is more useful than a copied list if it avoids sending players to impersonation communities.
